ElectroJetFlow

- Electrohydrodynamic Jet Flow Multiphysics Simulation

ElectroJetFlow was developed during my Ph.D. research to investigate electrohydrodynamic jets, electrospray atomization, and electrically driven multiphase flows. The project combines computational fluid dynamics, interface-capturing methods, electrostatics, and Maxwell-stress modelling to study jet formation, stability, breakup, and performance.
