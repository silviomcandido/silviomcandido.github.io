### Challenge
A muon collider needs a target that survives a multi-megawatt pulsed proton beam inside a superconducting solenoid of around 20 T. The strong field changes how a liquid metal flows, and nanosecond beam pulses send thermal-shock stress waves through a solid target.

### What I did
- **Proposed a compact liquid-lead jet target** with its collection pool, using magnetohydrodynamic CFD to quantify jet deformation, electromagnetic damping and stability in the 20 T field.
- **Designed a graphite target for 2–4 MW pulsed beam loads**: optimised the helium cooling (mass flow, annular gap, pressure drop), proposed a titanium flow-guiding vessel, and verified thermal stresses (ANSYS Mechanical) and thermal-shock stress waves from nanosecond pulses (explicit dynamics, LS-DYNA).

### Tools
OpenFOAM and ANSYS Fluent (MHD, VOF), ANSYS Mechanical, LS-DYNA, Python.
